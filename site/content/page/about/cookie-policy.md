<h2>What are cookies?</h2>
<p>"Cookies" are small text files that are stored on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website.</p>
<p>They allow the website to recognise your device and remember if you've been to the website before.</p>
<p>Cookies are very common web technology - most websites use cookies and have done for years. Cookies are widely used in order to make websites work more efficiently, as well as providing information to the owners of websites.</p>
<p>Cookies are used to measure which parts of the website people visit and to customise your experience. Cookies also provide information that helps us monitor and improve the website's performance.</p>
<p>You can read more about the different types of cookies that we use on this website on this page.</p>
<p>To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set on your computer and how to manage and delete them, visit&nbsp;<a href="http://www.allaboutcookies.org">www.allaboutcookies.org</a>.</p>
