<p><strong><u>Finding support</u></strong></p>
<p>We understand reporting abuse may be very difficult and that not everyone may want to contact the church directly. Below is a list of other agencies that are available to assist either on a 24-hour basis or through specialist helplines and services:</p>
<p><strong>Thirty One: Eight</strong>: 0303 003 1111 (out of hours and weekend safeguarding advice)</p>
<p><strong>NSPCC Child Protection Helpline:</strong>&nbsp;0808 800 5000 (lines free and open 24 hours). Phone if you are worried about a child.</p>
<p><strong>Child-line:</strong>&nbsp;0800 1111 (lines free and open 24 hours). Phone if you are a child or young person and are worried about anything.</p>
<p><strong>National Domestic Violence Helpline:</strong>&nbsp;0808 2000 247 (lines free and open 24 hours). Phone if you are experiencing domestic abuse.</p>
<p><strong>Samaritans Helpline:</strong>&nbsp;116 123 (open 24 hours). Phone if you feel you are struggling to cope and need someone to talk to.</p>
<p><strong>Action on Elder Abuse Helpline:</strong>&nbsp;080 8808 8141 (free phone Monday to Friday 9-5pm).</p>
