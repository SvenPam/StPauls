<p>Here, in Jon Byles Hardman's own words, is how he came to take it:</p>
<p>"Hi Mike.</p>
<p>We heard that you weren't feeling too good, so thought you might appreciate a smile.</p>
<p>Firstly, Milly's christening went very well, and the service was lovely. Many thanks to your "stand-in" on the day, who was brilliant. If you can let him know that the hot pot was excellent, and its a shame he couldn't join us....</p>
<p>Anyhow, completely unrelated, you may remember that it snowed a few weeks ago, and after our chat about me wanting to get a long exposure photograph of the church, I considered it a great opportunity.</p>
<p>I didn't get out of our house till quite late on, however, when I got to the church thee was a beautiful layer of snow on the ground, the roof, and a dusting on all the brickwork.&nbsp;The floodlights were lighting it up superbly, and the scene resembled the perfect Christmas scene for a Christmas card. You get the idea.</p>
<p>I set up my tripod, correct lens on camera, ISO and aperture settings checked, lens cap off, remote shutter release in hand, I raise my thumb to depress the shutter......and........ YOUR FLOODLIGHTS TURNED OFF !</p>
<p>I now realise that they must be on a timer, and 10.30 pm is their time for switching off.</p>
<p>I'll be honest, it made me laugh, despite the frostbitten fingers....</p>
<p>So, I ventured out last night. No snow I'm afraid, however I got a shot which I quite like.</p>
<p>It's on my Flickr page if you want to have a look.&nbsp;<a href="https://flic.kr/p/E4yYRf" target="_blank">https://flic.kr/p/E4yYRf</a></p>
<p>If you'd like a print of it, let me know,</p>
<p>And please feel free to put it on the website if you see fit.&nbsp;</p>
<p>As I'm a bit of a perfectionist, I've got another one, which is slightly lighter, so its up to you which you think works best. (sending separately, as Gmail doesnt like large files)</p>
<p>On a serious note, its a beautiful church, and I hope the pic does it justice. I hope to get some more soon, and would love to get some of the interior at some point.</p>
<p>Hope you are feeling better, and hope to speak soon</p>
<p>Thanks</p>
<p>Jon Byles Hardman"</p>
