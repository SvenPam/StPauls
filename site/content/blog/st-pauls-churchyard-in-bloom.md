<p>Once again the churchyard at St Paul's is in full bloom with the most beautiful crocus and snowdrops to the delight of passers by and local photographers (if not to the band of locals who tend to the churchyard!). Once these have died back then the primrose will be out in force so well worth a return visit in April or May.</p>
