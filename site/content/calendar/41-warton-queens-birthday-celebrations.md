Saturday 11th June 8.00pm.

Bryning with Warton Parish Council warmly invites you to join them in a FREE celebration of the Queen's birthday. There will be refreshments, live music, MP Mark Menzies lighting our beacon at 9.30 pm and a grand firework finale!

All at the Village Hall and all welcome.
