<p>The Braunschweig Youth Brass Band will be giving a free concert on Friday 29th July 2016 at 7:30pm at St. Paul's Church, Warton.</p>
<p>Braunschweig is the district in Germany that is home to our twinned parish of Timmerlah and Sonnenburg and these young people are currently on a tour of the UK.</p>
<p>Please come along and listen to this exceptional group of young people.</p>
