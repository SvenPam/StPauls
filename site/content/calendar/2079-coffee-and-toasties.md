Holy Trinity Church will be open on Saturday 8 April from 10:00 to 12:00.

We warmly welcome you to drop in if you’d like to view the church or just have a peaceful rest.

Tea, coffee and toasted sandwiches will be available for a donation, and proceeds will go to the charities that our Sunday School and Yurch group support.
