<p>Please note that due to Coronavirus restrictions the following rules will be in place for the safety of all involved. Please adhere to them at all times.</p>
<p>1. Face coverings are to be worn by all people not classed as exempt by Government rules at all times except when sitting down to have refreshments.<br>
2. Only mix with people in your household or support bubble.<br>
3. Entrance is via the church main door only.<br>
4. Give your contact details to a member of staff or scan the QR code for the NHS Contact Tracing app.<br>
5. Use the hand sanitizer provided.<br>
6. Please follow the one-way system around the stalls and keep 2 metres away from other people/groups.<br>
7. If having refreshments please find a table to sit at and wait to be served.<br>
8. Exit via the door next to the Vestry.</p>
<p>Thank you for your cooperation with making our Christmas Market safe and successful.</p>
