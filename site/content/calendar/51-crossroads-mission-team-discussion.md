Crossroads Mission Team Discussion led by the Bishop of Birkenhead

Noon-1:00pm Saturday September 10th

Lunch and discussion group regarding mission and future of the Church, with church members and non-church members.

Bring a sandwich, (bring a friend) tea and coffee provided and have your say about the plans of the Church, With Bishop Keith (Birkenhead) and the Crossroads mission team.
