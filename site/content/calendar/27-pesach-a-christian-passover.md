<p>A Christian Passover will be held in&nbsp;Freckleton Village Hall on&nbsp;Monday 8<sup>th</sup> April 2019. at 19.00.</p>
<p>Admission £7 per person&nbsp;(payable in advance) plus a donation on the night.</p>
<p>Proceeds to Holy Trinity Church&nbsp;so please give generously.</p>
<p>Please bring your own drinks for the meal.</p>
<p>"<strong>Passover </strong>or&nbsp;<strong>Pesach</strong>, is an important, biblically derived Jewish festival. The Jewish people celebrate Passover as a commemoration of their liberation by God from slavery in Egypt and their freedom as a nation under the leadership of Moses."</p>
