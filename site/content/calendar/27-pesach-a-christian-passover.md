A Christian Passover will be held in Freckleton Village Hall on Monday 8th April 2019. at 19.00.

Admission £7 per person (payable in advance) plus a donation on the night.

Proceeds to Holy Trinity Church so please give generously.

Please bring your own drinks for the meal.

"**Passover** or **Pesach**, is an important, biblically derived Jewish festival. The Jewish people celebrate Passover as a commemoration of their liberation by God from slavery in Egypt and their freedom as a nation under the leadership of Moses."
