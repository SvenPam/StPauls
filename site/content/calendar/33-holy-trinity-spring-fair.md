Holy Trinity Church are going to hold a spring fair at Freckleton C of E School on Saturday 16 April, opening at 14:00 with a performance by the school choir. If you would kindly like to donate any items, please deposit them at the back of church. Suitable donations may include bottles (e.g. wine, spirits, soft drinks or sauces), Sunday School tombola prizes (e.g. toys, chocolates or biscuits), good quality bric-a-brac, or cakes. We thank you for your generosity and hope you will join us on the 16th.
