Our friends at [Freckleton Methodist Church](http://www.freckleton-methodist-church.org.uk) have invited us to join them for a special evening of Songs of Praise on Sunday, 27th March 2016, Easter Sunday, with Revd. Sylvester Deigh.

The evening will start at 6:30pm and will be accompanied by members of the [Freckleton Band](http://freckletonband.co.uk).
