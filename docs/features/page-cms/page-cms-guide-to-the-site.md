# A guide to the new church website

This is for Paul and anyone at the church who wants to know what is on the new website and what has changed from the old one. It goes page by page, in the order of the top menu. Where it says "edit in", that is where you change it in Pages CMS. The [editing guide](page-cms-guide-for-editors.md) has the steps.

Most things look and work as before. The colours and layout are the same, with some tidying to make the text easier to read.

## What changed everywhere

- Visitors can't register or log in any more, and nobody edits on the website itself. You edit in Pages CMS, and the old "log in" and admin links now take you there.
- Comments on articles have gone.
- The email subscription has gone. Only three or four people ever signed up. Instead, the blog, the calendar and the notices each have an RSS feed, which people can follow in a news reader app.
- Google Analytics has gone, so nothing tracks visitors, and the website sets no cookies of its own.
- Pages are plain text with headings, lists, links and pictures. The "jump to" menus at the top of long pages have gone, and pictures now fit the width of the text rather than being set to a size.
- Notices and events drop off by themselves when they end.
- When someone shares a page on Facebook, the post shows its title, summary and picture. This starts working from switchover day.
- Long pages get a "Back to top" button once you scroll down.

## Home

The big church picture with the name and the line under it, then Church Notices, Upcoming Events and the three newest blog articles.

- **Edit in:** Home page (the picture, its description, the name and the line under it). Notices, events and articles come from their own sections.
- **Changed:** the picture now runs from edge to edge. The Notices section is hidden when there are no notices, and Upcoming Events when there are no events.

## Notices

Notices show on the home page and on the Notices page (linked in the footer). Each has its own page.

- **Edit in:** Notices.
- **Changed:** each notice has a "Show from" and an optional "Show until" date. The home page shows the five newest that have started. A notice appears or goes away overnight, or at the next save. A notice that has ended still has its page, with a box saying it has expired, but it drops off the lists.

## Calendar and events

The Calendar lists upcoming events, soonest first. Each event has its own page with its start and end, a countdown, the location and the details.

- **Edit in:** Events.
- **Changed:** times show as 24-hour (19:30). An event is marked "Soon" in the week before it starts (the old site only did this in the last 24 hours), "In Progress" while it's on, and drops off the calendar once it ends.

## Blog

Articles, newest first, with topics down the side on a computer.

- **Edit in:** Blog.
- **Changed:** each article shows its author ("by Paul Barfoot"). An article without its own picture borrows one of the church's photos for its card and for Facebook.

## About

About Us, Contact Us, the Privacy Policy and the Cookie Policy. The Church Diary and Contact List pages are linked from About Us.

- **Edit in:** Pages, About.
- **Changed:** the Cookie Policy and Privacy Policy still have the old wording, which mentions logins, comments and Google Analytics. Suggested new wording is in [policy updates](page-cms-policy-updates.md) for the church to agree.

The **Church Diary** page shows the church's Google calendar. Google may set a cookie when it loads.

## Services, Groups, Community, Links, Forms, Safeguarding

Ordinary pages, as before. A menu item with one page links straight to it. With more than one, it opens a short list.

- **Edit in:** Pages, then the group with the same name.
- **Changed:** nothing beyond the simpler text. The Safeguarding page stays as the Diocese requires.

## Gallery

Church Gallery shows photos from the church Facebook page. Website Pictures shows the church's own photos.

- **Edit in:** Pages, Gallery.
- **Changed:** nothing.

## Contact

The Contact Us page has the church address and contact list, and a form that emails the church at wartonstpauls@gmail.com. A visitor picks an enquiry type, such as Weddings and Baptisms, and sees a thank-you page after sending.

- **Edit in:** Pages, About, Contact Us (the words). Site settings (the enquiry types, the email subject and the address).
- **Changed:** the form stays hidden until it is switched on with a key in Site settings. Ste does this.

## Footer

At the bottom of every page: links to the main sections and pages, the map, the address and the Facebook link.

- **Edit in:** Site settings (the address, Facebook, map and directions links). A page joins the footer when you tick "Show in the footer" on it.
- **Changed:** the old footer's personal links have gone. The map is hidden on phones, as before.

## When changes show

- A save shows on the website about three minutes later.
- Notices that start or end on a date change overnight.
- There is no preview. Save, wait a few minutes, then check the page on the website.

## Switchover day

**Before the day.** You can practise in Pages CMS. It edits a test copy of the website at a test address. Keep making real changes on the old website until the switch.

**On the day.** Ste will agree a date and time with you and send a message the day before. At the agreed time, stop changing the old website. Everything on it is copied across to the new one, so anything added during practice is thrown away. Then the web address, stpaulswarton.co.uk, starts showing the new website. It stays the same address.

**After the day.** Pages CMS is the only place to edit. The old back office is switched off. Ste sends a message when it's done, and the [editing guide](page-cms-guide-for-editors.md) still applies.
